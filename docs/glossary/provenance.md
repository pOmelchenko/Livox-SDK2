# Provenance

- **Canonical term:** Provenance
- **Slug:** `provenance`
- **Aliases:** source attribution; origin
- **Russian:** происхождение

## Definition

Provenance is the documented origin, authorship, license, and immutable identity of material included in a change.

## Repository meaning and boundaries

Every downstream change records project-owned origin or complete third-party provenance and preserves inherited notices.

## Example

A third-party candidate records its repository, full commit SHA, author, license, and selected files.
